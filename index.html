The Legend of Zelda - Dungeon Recreation

A team project for EECS 494 recreating dungeon gameplay from The Legend of Zelda using Unity. The game includes enemies, weapons, collectibles, room exploration, and a custom level featuring a star power-up.

My contributions: I developed player health and health UI, damage feedback, hit-stun, knockback, temporary invincibility, and death/restart behavior. I worked on grid-based movement, controls, resolution settings, and cheats.

I implemented and refined Stalfos, Keese, Gel, Wallmaster, Blade Trap, and Aquamentus behavior. I also worked on collectibles, rupee UI, enemy drops, the old-man room and dialogue, doors, room transitions, and room-clear rewards. My integration and debugging work included boomerang stunning and item collection, projectile collisions with water, boss aiming, and trap activation.

Collaboration: This was a two-person project. My teammate developed the sword and weapon systems, star power-up, and custom level.

Technologies: Unity, C#, Git.

Download the game for Windows and Mac

Download and extract the ZIP, then open the folder for your operating system and launch the game.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, X for the sword, Z for the selected secondary weapon, and Space to cycle secondary weapons.

Original game and characters by Nintendo. Created as a student project.
